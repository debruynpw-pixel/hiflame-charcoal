About HiFlame

Crafted for the SA Braai Culture

Based in Delmas, Mpumalanga, HiFlame Charcoal is a proudly South African manufacturer of premium braai briquettes and industrial briquette-making machines. Founded by Hennie & Suzette, we've built our reputation on consistent quality, longer burn times, and a genuine love for the braai.

Premium Raw Materials
We use only high-quality carbonised biomass, ensuring a clean, hot, and consistent burn every single time.
Bulk Supply Ready
Whether you're a retailer, restaurant, or hospitality venue — we supply in bulk with reliable, on-time delivery.
Locally Owned, SA Proud
A subsidiary of Kingpin Engineering — rooted in Mpumalanga, committed to South African braai heritage.
